ZODI CHARACTER STYLE RULES

All ZODI fighters wear:
- Matte black luxury tracksuit
- Hoodie up in combat mode
- AI glasses over eyes called Z-VIS
- Z-VIS hides identity and gives tactical HUD
- Shoes inspired by a high-top basketball sneaker silhouette, similar energy to Jordan 1 style, but original ZODI design
- No capes
- No masks
- No childish superhero costumes

Z-VIS functions:
- Identity masking
- Threat detection
- Weak-point scan
- Combat guidance
- Team communication
- Emotional/stress monitoring

Kai / Pisces:
- 16 years old
- Quiet, creative, overlooked
- Power: illusions, dream projection, water-like movement
- Weakness: loses grip on reality under stress
- Combat look: hoodie up, deep blue glowing Z-VIS, soft Pisces energy

Nova / Aquarius:
- 17 years old
- Rebellious tech genius
- Power: electricity, hacking, signal control
- Weakness: sensory/data overload
- Combat look: electric-blue HUD, circuit-like suit glow

Ryder / Aries:
- 17 years old
- Athletic, impulsive, aggressive
- Power: speed bursts, impact force, shockwave punches
- Weakness: overheats when angry
- Combat look: red energy cracks, aggressive stance
