ZODI — EPISODE 1
“THE BREACH”

Genre: grounded teen sci-fi superhero drama
Length target: 8–12 minute pilot short
Tone: realistic, cinematic, dark, premium, emotional

COLD OPEN
SCENE 1 — EXT. CITY SKYLINE — NIGHT

A modern city sleeps under rain and neon.

A black glass corporate tower stands above everything.

On the building:

ZODI

A luxury tech corporation. Clean. Powerful. Untouchable.

VOICEOVER — DR. ELIAN VALE
Most people think identity is something you’re born with.
But identity can be activated.

Cut to black.

ACT 1 — THE TECHNOLOGY
SCENE 2 — INT. ZODI LAB — NIGHT

A high-security underground lab.

Scientists move around twelve glass display chambers.

Inside each chamber: a matte black luxury tracksuit.

Each suit has a subtle zodiac symbol on the chest.

Not costumes.

Premium streetwear.

Expensive. Minimal. Dangerous.

DR. ELIAN VALE, 50s, controlled and brilliant, watches from behind glass.

Beside him is MAYA CROSS, 30s, head of ZODI security.

MAYA
You said they were wearable prototypes.

DR. VALE
They were. Yesterday.

One suit pulses.

The Pisces symbol glows faint blue.

MAYA
And today?

DR. VALE
Today they started choosing.

Maya looks disturbed.

MAYA
Choosing who?

Vale does not answer.

SCENE 3 — INT. ZODI LAB / TEST ROOM — NIGHT

A test dummy stands in the center.

A phone scans the symbol on one suit.

The suit breaks apart into thousands of black particles and wraps around the dummy.

Hood up.

AI glasses form over the eyes.

The lenses glow with a clean digital HUD.

Z-VIS ONLINE

SCIENTIST
Neural sync stable.

DR. VALE
Combat mode.

The suit tightens.

The shoes lock into place — black high-top sneakers with a Jordan 1-style silhouette, but original ZODI design.

The dummy punches forward.

A shockwave cracks the wall.

Everyone freezes.

MAYA
That was at ten percent?

SCIENTIST
Eight.

Vale smiles, but not with joy.

With fear.

ACT 2 — THE ATTACK
SCENE 4 — EXT. ZODI TOWER — NIGHT

A black van stops in an alley.

Six masked intruders step out.

No wasted movement.

Professional.

Their leader, called VANT, wears a dark tactical coat.

He looks up at ZODI tower.

VANT
We take the suits. No witnesses.

They enter through an underground service door.

SCENE 5 — INT. ZODI SECURITY HALLWAY — NIGHT

The lights flicker.

ZODI security guards notice too late.

The intruders move fast.

Silent takedowns.

Security alarms trigger.

Red lights flood the halls.

SYSTEM VOICE
Security breach. Sector Seven.

Maya hears it through comms.

MAYA
Lock down the vault. Now.

SCENE 6 — INT. ZODI LAB — NIGHT

Scientists panic.

The twelve suits begin reacting.

Symbols glow:

Aries red.
Taurus green.
Gemini yellow.
Cancer silver.
Leo gold.
Virgo white.
Libra violet.
Scorpio crimson.
Sagittarius orange.
Capricorn steel.
Aquarius electric blue.
Pisces deep blue.

SCIENTIST
They’re waking up.

DR. VALE
No. They’re defending themselves.

The doors explode open.

Vant and his team enter.

Gunfire. Sparks. Screaming.

Maya and ZODI security fire back.

The lab becomes chaos.

SCENE 7 — INT. ZODI LAB — CONTINUOUS

Vant walks toward the suit chambers.

He pulls out a black device.

VANT
Transfer ownership.

The device scans the suits.

The suits reject him.

A violent energy pulse throws him back.

VANT
They locked them.

DR. VALE
They were never yours.

Vant aims at Vale.

VANT
Then I’ll take the system apart.

He fires at the main control core.

The core cracks.

Everything goes unstable.

The twelve chambers open.

The suits dissolve into glowing black particles.

Each suit compresses into a small zodiac emblem patch.

The patches shoot out like meteors through the ceiling and into the city.

Maya watches in horror.

MAYA
Where are they going?

Vale whispers:

DR. VALE
To the chosen.

Cut to black.

Title card:

ZODI
ACT 3 — THE TEEN
SCENE 8 — INT. HIGH SCHOOL — MORNING

Loud hallway.

Students laugh, shove, flirt, argue.

Everything feels normal.

KAI TRAN, 16, quiet and observant, moves through the crowd with headphones on.

He carries a sketchbook.

Nobody really notices him.

A kid bumps his shoulder hard.

Kai’s sketchbook falls.

Drawings scatter.

One drawing shows a hooded figure with glowing eyes.

A popular student, RYDER COLE, picks it up.

Ryder is athletic, confident, reckless.

RYDER
Yo, this is actually sick.

Kai reaches for it.

KAI
Can I get that back?

Ryder looks at him.

Not cruel. Just intense.

RYDER
You draw superheroes?

KAI
No.

Beat.

KAI
People hiding.

Ryder stares for a second, then hands it back.

RYDER
That’s dark, bro.

Ryder walks away.

Kai watches him.

SCENE 9 — INT. CLASSROOM — DAY

Teacher talks. Students half-listen.

Kai sketches under the desk.

He draws the same symbol again and again.

Two fish circling.

Pisces.

He stops.

He does not know why he drew it.

His phone glitches.

For one second, the screen shows:

SCAN TO SYNC

Kai blinks.

The screen returns to normal.

SCENE 10 — EXT. SCHOOL — AFTERNOON

Rain begins.

Kai walks home alone.

He cuts through a narrow alley behind a closed shopping plaza.

He hears something metallic hit the ground.

A small black patch lies near a drain.

It has the Pisces symbol.

Same symbol from his drawing.

Kai picks it up.

The patch feels warm.

His phone turns on by itself.

Camera opens.

SCAN DETECTED

Kai whispers:

KAI
What the hell…

The phone scans the patch.

Everything goes silent.

ACT 4 — FIRST ACTIVATION
SCENE 11 — EXT. ALLEY — CONTINUOUS

The patch liquefies into black particles.

They crawl up Kai’s arm.

He panics.

KAI
No, no, no—

The particles spread over his chest, legs, shoulders.

A matte black tracksuit forms around him.

Premium. Clean. Perfectly fitted.

The hoodie rises over his head by itself.

Black AI glasses form across his eyes.

The lenses glow deep blue.

Inside the glasses, Kai sees a HUD.

Z-VIS ONLINE
USER: UNREGISTERED
ZODI SIGNATURE: PISCES
SYNC: 12%

Kai breathes hard.

KAI
Take it off. Take it off!

The system responds in a calm AI voice.

Z-VIS
Panic detected. Stabilizing.

Blue energy pulses through his suit.

The alley bends visually.

The rain freezes mid-air.

Kai sees impossible images:

Water flowing upward.
A shadow version of himself.
A city underwater.
A girl screaming in a hallway he has never seen.

Then everything snaps back.

Kai falls to his knees.

The suit retracts into the patch on his hoodie.

He is back in normal clothes.

The Pisces patch remains.

Small. Black. Hidden.

Kai runs.

ACT 5 — FIRST CRIME
SCENE 12 — EXT. CORNER STORE — NIGHT

Kai stands outside a corner store, still shaken.

He tries to peel off the patch.

It will not come off.

Inside the store, two masked thieves threaten the cashier.

Kai sees them.

He freezes.

One thief shoves the cashier.

Kai backs away.

He is scared.

Then he hears a young kid crying inside.

Kai looks down at the patch.

His phone vibrates.

THREAT DETECTED

He whispers:

KAI
I can’t do this.

The patch glows.

Z-VIS
Combat response available.

Kai looks at the store.

Then at his reflection in the glass.

Just a scared teenager.

KAI
Activate.

SCENE 13 — INT. CORNER STORE — NIGHT

The thieves grab cash.

The lights flicker.

The door opens.

Kai enters.

Hood up.

Black tracksuit.

Z-VIS glowing over his eyes.

One thief turns.

THIEF #1
Yo, what are you supposed to be?

Kai says nothing.

Z-VIS scans.

Z-VIS
Two attackers. One blade. One firearm replica. Probability of injury: high.

Kai’s hands shake.

The thief rushes him.

Kai flinches.

Blue energy bursts out.

The thief suddenly sees the floor turn into water.

He slips, screaming.

It is not real.

But his brain believes it.

The second thief swings a bat.

Kai moves like water, almost phasing sideways.

He dodges without understanding how.

KAI
I didn’t do that.

Z-VIS
Correction. You did.

The second thief runs.

Kai raises his hand.

A blue illusion wall appears in front of the door.

The thief crashes into it.

The cashier stares.

The kid stares.

Kai panics and runs out.

ACT 6 — CONSEQUENCES
SCENE 14 — EXT. ROOFTOP — NIGHT

Kai lands hard on a rooftop.

He did not mean to jump that high.

He tears off the glasses, but they dissolve back into the suit.

He looks over the city.

Terrified.

Powerful.

Alone.

KAI
Who made this?

Z-VIS glitches.

A hidden file opens.

A broken video message appears.

Dr. Vale’s face flickers.

DR. VALE — RECORDING
If you are seeing this, the suit has chosen you.
Do not trust ZODI.
Do not trust anyone looking for the suits.
And whatever you do—

The message glitches.

DR. VALE — RECORDING
Find the others before he does.

The video cuts.

Kai hears sirens in the distance.

Then another sound.

A drone.

He looks up.

A black surveillance drone watches him.

Its red lens locks on.

Z-VIS
Warning. You have been found.

Cut to black.

END OF EPISODE 1
Final shot after credits
SCENE 15 — INT. DARK ROOM — UNKNOWN LOCATION

Vant watches footage of Kai on a screen.

The Pisces symbol flashes.

Vant smiles.

VANT
One woke up.

Behind him, a wall displays eleven empty suit slots.

VANT
Bring me the boy.

Cut to black.
